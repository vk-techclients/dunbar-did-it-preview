The Process

How buying a Houston home works

The process typically involves five steps — and Brandon walks each one with you:

1

Pre-Approval

Getting pre-approved for a mortgage, so your offer carries weight from day one.

2

The Search

Working with a realtor to find properties — Brandon’s knowledge of the Houston market is unmatched.

3

Offer, Inspection, Appraisal

Making an offer, then home inspection and appraisal, with expert guidance at every signature.

Then comes closing — step five, and the keys. Financing options include conventional mortgages, FHA loans, and VA loans — and Texas offers special programs for first-time buyers through the Texas Department of Housing.
